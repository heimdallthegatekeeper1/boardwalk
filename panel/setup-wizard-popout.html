What this page can and cannot do. Every control here is a request. open: takes you to another surface · grant: queues an ASK in the timed-grant ledger and shows you the exact sudoers line before anything is issued · recheck: and step: re-measure. No button here runs sudo, installs software, or writes a secret. Root blocks by default and a grant is an exception with a clock on it.

★ It may ASK. It may not TAKE. — the operator's own ruling, 2026-10-09.
